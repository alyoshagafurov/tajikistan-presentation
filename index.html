Tajikistan

A personal introduction by Abdullo Gafurov.

The Pamirs

A homeland shaped by mountains.

Rivers from the ice

Mountain water travels into fertile valleys.

A lake of legends

Still water beneath the Fann Mountains.

A history written in stone

Hissar Fortress has watched over this valley for generations.

Sarazm: ancient life

An ancient settlement and place of long-distance exchange.

Dushanbe, our capital

A lively city among the mountains.

A city of meeting places

Rudaki Park is a favorite place to stroll and gather.

Dushanbe after dark

The capital takes on another rhythm at night.

Welcome, Navruz

Families greet spring with food, music and new beginnings.

A table for everyone

Qurutob is a favorite dish made to be shared.

The plate at the center

Palov brings family and friends around the table.

And bread for the table

Fresh non is part of every welcome.

Patterns with meaning

Embroidery and handmade crafts keep tradition close.

Strings that travel

Rubab and dutar give Tajik music its distinctive sound.

Falak: a voice to the sky

Mountain songs passed from one generation to the next.

My home, your home

I’m happy to share my country with you here in China.

Thank you

I hope you remember the mountains — and our welcome.
