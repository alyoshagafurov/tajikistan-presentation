## Generated artwork

- `assets/pamir-cover.png` was generated for this presentation. Its full image prompt is embedded in the PNG metadata.
- `assets/pamir-hero-generated.png` and `assets/tajik-music-generated.png` are older generated assets retained in the project but not used in this deck. Their original prompts were not present in the supplied project history.

## Video and anthem recording

- `assets/dushanbe-at-night.mp4` and `assets/tandoor-bread.mp4` were supplied by the presenter. The Dushanbe night video is used behind the anthem player; the bread video remains unused.
- `assets/tajik-national-anthem.mp3` is a local copy of the National Anthem recording published by the [Embassy of Tajikistan in Qatar](https://tajembqatar.tj/video/). The presentation attributes the anthem’s lyrics to Gulnazar Keldi and music to Suleiman Yudakov. No separate reuse license was stated on the source page.
