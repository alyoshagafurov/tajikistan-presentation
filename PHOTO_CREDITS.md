# Image and video notes

The photos and videos supplied by Abdullo Gafurov are included in `assets/`. They are used as provided, with cropping and simple layered frames in the presentation. Please retain this note when sharing the project.

## User-supplied media

- Tajik street, monument, flags, traditional clothing, tea, and palov images: supplied by the presenter.
- Dushanbe at night (`dushanbe-at-night.mp4`) and bread baking (`tandoor-bread.mp4`): supplied by the presenter.
- The meal image (`uzbek-table.jpg`) and craft video (`central-asian-craft.mp4`) are identified as Uzbek material and are labeled as a neighbor-country comparison.
- `heritage-from-above.mp4` shows an unidentified historic site. The presentation does not assign it a specific place.
